## Feedback points

### S1 Motivation and Scientific Background (8.0)
- Research gap and use case are clear, with relevant literature and links to other course topics.
- **Improve:** explain more explicitly how the approach complements or differs from related topics, particularly **decision mining** and **predictive process monitoring**, instead of mainly stating similarities.

### S2 Conceptual Approach (8.0)
- Clearly structured; main methodological steps and choices are well explained.
- **Improve:** connect the chosen classification approach and the **throughput-time cutoff** more explicitly to the interpretation of the resulting rules and the conclusions that can be drawn from them.

### S3 Implementation (10.0)
- No access to the implementation, so they could not check that the description matches it.
- Based on the information given, the approach appears reproducible.
- No action points.

### S4 Evaluation Design and Results (8.0)
- Thorough and clearly presented, with appropriate validation procedures and comparisons.
- **Improve:** distinguish more carefully between findings directly supported by the experiments and possible explanations for the limited predictive performance, particularly the influence of **deviation features versus model selection and tuning**.

### S5 Limitations and Future Work (8.0)
- Limitations are relevant, clearly explained and linked to future work.
- **Improve:** highlight which limitations have the greatest impact on the interpretation and reliability of the findings, so the important concerns separate from the minor ones.

### S6 Report Quality, Structure and Appearance (6.0)
- **Page limit exceeded** by roughly one third of a page.
- **Main issue:** missing context before technical terminology is introduced and before the methodology is explained.
- **Spelling mistakes** and **unfinished sentences**.
