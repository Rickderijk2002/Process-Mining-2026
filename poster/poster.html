Group 8: Rick de Rijk, Thom Verzantvoort, Stefan Vonk, Tycho van Rooij

Motivation and Scientific Background

  • Use case. A Dutch bank (BPI Challenge 2017) denied 3,093 loan applications, in under a day or after about 260 days.
  • Question. Which deviations from the normative process go with slow denials? Allow or forbid them?
  • Why not flag all deviations? Some are workarounds linked to better performance (Dees et al., 2017).
  • Course fit. Resource and temporal conformance check text constraints; we check control flow against a model.

Limitations and Future Work

Limitations

  • Two-rule tree is unstable across splits.
  • Root split hits 87% of traces: net may be too strict.
  • One cutoff, one dataset, control flow only.

Future work

  • Regression tree on continuous throughput time.
  • Sample several optimal alignments per trace.
  • Data-aware checking (guards such as loan amount).
